Leadership & Vision

Sri Sai Kumar

Founder & Managing Director, Saikumar Computer Training Institute

Welcome to SAIKUMAR COMPUTER TRAINING INSTITUTE. Founded with the conviction that quality technology education should be accessible, practical, and affordable for every student in Andhra Pradesh, our institute has grown into a premier hub for digital empowerment.

Having observed that traditional college curriculums often lack hands-on application, I established this institute to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real corporate IT demands. Here, we emphasize 80% practical lab work, individual computer desks, and direct mentorship.

"Our motto, 'Learn Today, Lead Tomorrow', is more than a slogan. It is our promise to prepare each student with the technical mastery, confidence, and ethics needed to succeed in India's booming digital economy."

Our Journey

Built on Trust, Excellence & Innovation

Starting as a specialized computer learning center in Kurnool, SAIKUMAR COMPUTER TRAINING INSTITUTE has expanded its training footprint to serve students from across Rayalaseema and coastal Andhra Pradesh, including Kurnool, Proddatur (YSR Kadapa Dist), Kadapa, and Vijayawada.

We recognized that students needed more than rote memorization; they required live coding experience, real GST accounting ledger practice on Tally Prime, interactive web design builds, and actionable data analytics. Today, our labs are equipped with high-speed computers, dual monitors, licensed software, and continuous high-speed optical internet.

Our Purpose

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Guiding principles that define our educational philosophy every single day.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, practical, and employment-focused computer training to students, job seekers, and professionals in Andhra Pradesh at affordable fees in Indian Rupees, enabling them to achieve digital self-reliance and lucrative careers.

Our Vision

To be recognized as Andhra Pradesh's most trusted and result-driven computer training institution, fostering a generation of proficient software engineers, data analysts, accountants, and digital entrepreneurs who drive technological advancement.

Our Core Values

Integrity, individual student attention, continuous curriculum updating to reflect global tech trends, relentless practical practice, and genuine student welfare over commercial profit.

How We Teach

Our 4-Stage Teaching Methodology

Designed to transform complete beginners into confident professionals.

01

Concept Clarity

Interactive whiteboard & screen projector explanations breaking down complex coding or accounting concepts into simple terms.

02

1:1 Lab Hands-On

Every student implements what they learn on their personal assigned workstation immediately with lab faculty standing by.

03

Real-World Capstones

Students create live websites, commercial accounting books with GST, and data dashboards solving actual business scenarios.

04

Assessment & Certification

Comprehensive practical examination, viva, code review, and issuance of government recognized ISO 9001:2015 certificates.
